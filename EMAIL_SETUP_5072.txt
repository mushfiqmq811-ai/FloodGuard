FloodGuard BD — Google Apps Script email setup

The app is preconfigured with the Web App URL created for this project.
For Render, you can optionally set:
  FLOODGUARD_EMAIL_WEBAPP_URL=<your Apps Script /exec URL>

The app sends:
1) one welcome email after alerts are enabled,
2) one email when the user's zone risk level actually changes,
3) one daily summary per calendar day,
4) a manual test email from My account & alerts.

Keep the Apps Script deployed as a Web app, Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone.
Do not put Google passwords or secrets in the website code.
